Twilio: The SOAR Accreditation Framework & Gong Telemetry

Role: Sales Enablement Leader Outcome: SOAR Accreditation Framework • Gong Telemetry

Traditional enablement relies on multiple-choice quizzes that test what reps can memorize, not what they can execute on live buyer calls. Twilio needed an observable competency framework that certified sellers on customer discovery and value articulation.

The Architectural Fix:

1. SOAR Framework: Architected a 5-module seller curriculum (Sales Outcomes Achieve Results) structured around observable behaviors: Discovery, Technical Validation, Economic Alignment, Closing, and Account Growth.
2. Gong AI Telemetry: Connected certification criteria to conversation intelligence trackers, measuring live-call talk ratios, silence adherence, and competitor displacement talk-tracks.
3. Measurable Impact: Connected accreditation criteria to field observation and conversation-intelligence signals to strengthen coaching consistency and adoption.
