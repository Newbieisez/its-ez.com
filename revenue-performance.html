THE PERFORMANCE CHAIN
ACTIVITY↓ENGAGEMENT↓SKILL EXECUTION↓BEHAVIOR↓FUNNEL↓PIPELINE QUALITY↓REVENUE↓RETENTION + GROWTH

Do not manage from the last number in the chain. Work backward until you find the first meaningful leak.

THE POINTOutcomes tell you what happened.
Diagnostics tell you why.

Quota, revenue, win rate, NRR and forecast accuracy matter. They are not a diagnosis. Good revenue leadership connects outcomes to leading indicators, observable behaviors, context and a specific action someone can take.

THE RULES

Do not let a dashboard make you stupid.

Metrics should sharpen judgment, not replace it. These are the operating guardrails behind the entire system.

01

Never coach activity before checking quality and conversion.

02

Never blame a rep before checking data, territory, ICP, product, pricing, process and manager effects.

03

Never declare training success from completion alone.

04

Never declare AI success from usage alone.

05

Never declare content success from downloads alone.

06

Never declare pipeline success from total dollars alone.

07

Never declare a manager successful from team quota alone.

08

Never declare customer health from sentiment alone.

09

Never declare partner enablement successful from certification alone.

10

Never present modeled revenue influence as realized revenue.

11

Always segment before acting and preserve source provenance.

12

Every alert needs an owner, action, SLA and measurement window.
