Senior sales enablement leader, AI workflow architect, and commercial systems engineer with 15+ years of verified revenue impact.

The Enablement Philosophy: Value Density Over Page Count

Most enablement departments operate as slide-deck agencies: they produce hundreds of pages of training material that reps ignore, run bootcamps that fail to change live call behavior, and measure 'vanity metrics' like course completion rates while revenue leaders wonder why deals slip.

EZ Enablement LLC was built on a different premise: Make It Useful. If a rubric doesn't remove subjectivity from a manager's deal inspection, don't write it. If a roleplay doesn't prepare a rep for a skeptical CFO, don't run it. If an AI prompt produces generic marketing fluff, delete it.
