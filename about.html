Why EZ?

Enablement should be easier to understand, easier to use, and easier to measure.

The name is simple on purpose. The work is not about producing more content. It is about reducing the distance between knowing what good looks like and being able to perform it consistently.

01 / KNOW

Clarify the move

Make the strategy, buyer, process, skill, or workflow understandable.

02 / PRACTICE

Rehearse it

Use realistic practice before the behavior is needed in the business.

03 / PROVE

Demonstrate readiness

Use observable evidence instead of assuming completion equals capability.

04 / PERFORM

Inspect the result

Look for changed behavior, better execution, and business outcomes over time.
