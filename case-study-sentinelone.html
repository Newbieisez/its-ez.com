Enterprise Case Study

SentinelOne: Consolidating $700K in Tool Sprawl & Engineering the Purple Book

Role: Sr. Manager Content & Systems Enablement Outcome: $700K Tooling Consolidation • Global Enablement Program

At SentinelOne, sales reps faced cognitive friction from fragmented knowledge repositories across Highspot, Google Drive, internal wikis, and Slack channels. Meanwhile, software license spend had ballooned across redundant enablement platforms.

The Architectural Fix:

1. Stack Consolidation: Audited 14 sales software contracts, eliminated redundant licenses, and consolidated $700,000 in annual recurring tooling costs into a single integrated Highspot, Salesforce, and Gong stack.
2. The Purple Book: Designed a centralized deal intelligence dashboard providing just-in-time competitive battlecards, MEDDPICC qualification rubrics, and pricing calculators directly in seller workflow.
3. Partnerstruck SKO: Created and launched a gamified sales kickoff certification curriculum for reps and partners globally.
