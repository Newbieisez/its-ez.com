THE LEARNING PATH

Four levels.
One useful progression.

Start where the work actually is. You do not need agents when the team still struggles to ground a prompt, and you do not need more prompts when the real problem is process design.

LEVEL 1

Use AI Well

Build the fundamentals before trying to automate anything.

  • Choose the right tool
  • Prompt for outcomes
  • Ground with approved sources
  • Organize projects and context
  • Verify before acting
  • Know when not to use AI
LEVEL 2

Build Better Workflows

Turn isolated prompts into repeatable work that saves time.

  • Research workflows
  • Meeting prep
  • Writing and docs
  • Spreadsheets and data
  • Presentations
  • Reusable templates
LEVEL 3

Build Systems

Create governed automation around clearly defined work.

  • AI agents
  • Automations
  • Connectors and MCP
  • Human approval gates
  • QA and guardrails
  • Multi-step workflows
LEVEL 4

Operationalize AI

Scale what works across teams and measure business value.

  • AI adoption
  • Team standards
  • Measurement
  • Workflow redesign
  • Governance
  • Scale proven patterns

THE EZ RULES

Better AI starts
before the prompt.

The quality of the system comes from clarity, context, and judgment—not from adding more technology.

1

Define the outcome

State what useful looks like before asking AI to do anything.

2

Ground the work

Use approved files, notes, systems, evidence, and source material.

3

Verify before acting

Check facts, calculations, assumptions, permissions, and recommendations before execution.
