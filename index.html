The operating model

Know. Practice. Prove. Perform.

Completion is not the goal. The standard is whether somebody can understand the move, rehearse it, demonstrate it, and then use it when the business actually needs it.

01 / KNOW

Understand the move.

Clarify the buyer, business problem, value, process, product, or methodology the field needs to understand.

Evidence: knowledge benchmark + message recall
02 / PRACTICE

Rehearse the workflow.

Use scenarios, discovery, objections, demos, roleplay, coaching, and realistic practice before the skill is needed live.

Evidence: observed practice + manager feedback
03 / PROVE

Demonstrate readiness.

Certify against observable criteria so readiness means demonstrated capability rather than content completion.

Evidence: performance score + readiness threshold
04 / PERFORM

Use it in the business.

Inspect live opportunities, partner motions, CRM behavior, calls, and business outcomes to see whether the capability transferred.

Evidence: behavior + pipeline + revenue movement
0–30 daysAdoption

Participation, practice, usage, certification starts, manager engagement, and baseline signals.

31–60 daysBehavior

Observed field execution, methodology use, coaching follow-through, content use, and workflow consistency.

61–90 daysPerformance

Opportunity quality, conversion movement, readiness, ramp, stage progression, and productivity.

90–180+ daysBusiness impact

Revenue, win rate, sales-cycle movement, ramp trend, partner contribution, savings, and sustained adoption.
