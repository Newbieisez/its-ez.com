HOW THE STORE SHOULD FEEL

Understand first.
Purchase second.

Every product detail page should show the outcome, the exact contents, who it is for, delivery format, license, compatibility, support and price before asking for payment.

01 · Inspect

See the actual product.

Real artwork, screenshots, sample structure and clear deliverables make the purchase tangible.

02 · Configure

Choose only where choice matters.

Deal Labs gets a configurator. Products with one clear package should stay simple.

03 · Buy

Checkout only when ready.

Live purchase controls appear only after pricing, fulfillment, licensing and entitlement are fully verified.
