The Human Judgment Loop

A repeatable 7-step operating model that turns generative AI drafts into buyer-ready, high-converting commercial work.

1. Intent
Define the exact commercial business purpose, target persona, and desired buying decision before opening an LLM.
2. Input
Provide verified ground-truth sources, account context, and constraints. Never ask AI to invent facts.
3. Generate
Prompt the AI to synthesize, format, or extract—never to hallucinate unverified ground truth.
4. Inspect
Screen against the 6-Point Inspection Checklist: Claim, Source, Specificity, Risk, Voice, and Action.
5. Verify
Cross-check metrics, dates, and competitive claims against primary source evidence.
6. Refine
Strip robotic filler, eliminate generic pleasantries, and inject authentic practitioner point of view.
7. Decide
Final human ownership: Approve & Ship, Hold for Evidence, Escalate to Leadership, or Reject & Re-prompt.
