Why music belongs here

CREATIVITY IS PART OF THE SYSTEM.

I don't see music as separate from the rest of the work. A strong experience has pacing. It has emotional shifts. It creates anticipation. It knows when to be loud and when to get out of the way. Those same instincts show up in the systems, learning experiences, events, and digital products I build.

03 / PacingBuild tension and release intentionally.Good learning and good music both understand timing.
04 / CraftUse every medium available.If audio makes the experience better, it belongs in the toolkit.
