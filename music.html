The idea behind it

CREATIVITY
IS PART OF THE SYSTEM.

Music and experience design use the same raw materials: pacing, tension, contrast, memory and release. The medium changes. The instinct does not.

01 / EnergySet the room before the message starts.Sound establishes momentum before a presenter says a word.
02 / MemoryMake the experience stick.The strongest moments leave a feeling, not only a slide deck.
03 / PacingBuild tension and release on purpose.Good learning and good music both understand timing.
04 / CraftUse every medium that improves the moment.If audio makes the experience stronger, it belongs in the toolkit.
