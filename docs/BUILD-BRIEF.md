## 1. Architectural Architecture
This codebase is a clean, static, high-performance HTML/CSS/JavaScript web ecosystem with no heavy compilation dependencies or build locks. It serves both the primary corporate presence of `its-ez.com` and the commercial digital storefront of `EZ Enablement LLC`.

## 2. Core Operating Principles
- **Store Standard Compliance:** Every product in `store.html` satisfies the 8 mandatory store criteria (Buyer, Problem, Outcome, Deliverables, Time, Delivery Method, Value Justification, Next Action).
- **Dual-Path Conversion:** Instant digital download checkout for software/rubrics ($297–$1,497) and structured qualification booking for high-ticket consulting sprints ($15,000–$25,000).
- **Separation of Concerns:** ENABLAYER is a separate software business. No Enablayer products or IP are combined with EZ Enablement commercial products.
- **Zero Generic AI Filler:** All content is grounded in real GTM enablement telemetry from SentinelOne, Twilio, Tessian, and Cofense.
